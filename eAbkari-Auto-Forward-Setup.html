eAbkari Auto Forward — one-time setup

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + B so the bookmarks bar is visible.
  2. Drag this green button up onto the bookmarks bar:

    Auto Forward

  3. Done. You can close this file.

Daily use

  1. Log in to eAbkari as usual.
  2. Permit/Pass → TP-PL Retail → Act On Application, set dates, click Search.
  3. Click Auto Forward on the bookmarks bar.
  4. A black box shows progress. Keep the tab open. Press STOP any time.
  5. When it says Done, press F5 to refresh the list.

Requisitions the site refuses (e.g. "stock unavailable") are skipped automatically and stay in the list.

Don't open eAbkari in a second tab while it runs.
